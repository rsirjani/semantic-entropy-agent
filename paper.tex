\paragraph{Registered headline endpoint: a strictly positive injectable window.}
We elevate the generator/outcome separation to the paper's single
\emph{confirmatory} endpoint, so that the contribution is differentiated from the
closest prior~\citep{bigelow2024forking} at the level of the claim rather than the
setting. Bigelow fits one Bayesian change-point on the answer distribution and
summarizes it by a single survival function $S(T)$, which has no axis on which a
generator-collapse prefix and an outcome-lock prefix can differ; our object is the
\emph{width} of the gap between them, $W=f^\dagger-f^\ast$, the injectable window in
resolved-entropy fraction. On the \emph{same} matched-$M$ resamples per prefix we
estimate two collapse points---$f^\ast$, the last prefix fraction at which the
model's own continuation distribution still occupies more than one class
($|\mathrm{supp}_\pi(k)|>1$), and $f^\dagger$, the last at which the executed
behavioral-signature distribution under a forced-diverse next action still does
($|\mathrm{R}(k)|>1$)---both by \emph{sustained} collapse (last upcrossing, F2),
never a first crossing. The pre-registered confirmatory endpoint is that the
\emph{paired median} of the per-instance gap $W$ is strictly positive across the
easy band, tested by a paired sign/Wilcoxon test on $\{f^\dagger_i-f^\ast_i\}$ with
per-instance bootstrap CIs on each threshold; we additionally report the fraction of
instances with a strictly positive window. The ordering $f^\ast\le f^\dagger$ is
guaranteed structurally by the support inclusion
$\mathrm{supp}_\pi(k)\subseteq\mathrm{R}(k)$ (plain resampling is one admissible
intervention, F4), so the test is one-sided and any observed inversion would signal
an estimator bug, not an effect; the substance of the endpoint is the strict
\emph{magnitude} $W>0$, i.e.\ that the gap does not degenerate to a point. The
falsifier is correspondingly sharp: if the paired median of $f^\dagger-f^\ast$ is
statistically indistinguishable from $0$ (its CI covers $0$), generator collapse and
outcome lock coincide, the window collapses to a point, and our two-threshold account
reduces exactly to Bigelow's single survival curve---the novelty is then refuted, not
merely weakened. The lone completed run cannot exercise this endpoint: it fixes only
$f^\ast$, and degenerately ($f^\ast=0$, $\widehat{H}_{\mathrm{MM}}=0$ at every measured
fraction, $n\!=\!1$ on \texttt{sympy-22714}), while $f^\dagger$ awaits the
forced-diverse reachable-support estimator that is not yet implemented; the median-$W$
test is therefore registered with $n=0$ usable gaps and stands pre-registered, not
measured.
